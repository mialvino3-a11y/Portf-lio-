About me

I'm a social scientist who works with data. I like to understand what lies behind each question and to find, in the data, ways to answer it, whether through an analysis, a dashboard, an indicator or an AI-powered solution.

The Social Sciences taught me to look at the context before the numbers, and my teaching degree taught me to explain complex things in a way everyone can understand. I've brought this approach to topics such as early childhood, social assistance, education and women's rights, and I'm open to new challenges, in the social or private sector.

01 · São Paulo State Government · Social assistance

SuperAção SP Program

Role
  • Built management dashboards in Power BI and Streamlit to track the program's implementation.
  • Built a monitoring portal in Streamlit that brings together, in one place, information from several areas of the implementation.
  • Designed monitoring metrics based on longitudinal studies and exploratory analyses of the program's data.
  • Analyzed the time series of the program's indicators, interpreting their evolution in light of the socio-historical context and implementation across territories.
  • Conducted technical studies that supported strategic decisions for the program, using descriptive statistics (mean, median and dispersion analysis) and outlier detection to identify values outside the expected pattern.
Tools
Power BIStreamlitPythonpandasDescriptive statisticsTime seriesOutlier detection
Impact
An integrated view of the program's implementation, with evidence-based decisions and dashboards tailored to management's real needs.

02 · Piauí State Government · Early childhood

Piauí Early Childhood Observatory

Role
  • Took part in business meetings to translate the client's needs into data and visualization requirements.
  • Based on the Nurturing Care Framework, which looks at child development holistically, brought together in a single Power BI view the main indicators of five areas: health, nutrition, education, social assistance and safety.
  • Built this integrated view even without a system connecting each area's databases, designing a navigation that lets users explore all areas in one place.
  • Proposed an early childhood vulnerability index that combines the five areas to identify the municipalities in Piauí where children face the worst outcomes across several dimensions at once.
  • Expanded the dashboard with new public data, such as vaccination coverage, collected through automated web scraping in Python: HTTP requests to official portals, HTML parsing with BeautifulSoup and standardization with pandas, producing indicators aligned with national benchmarks.
Tools
Power BIPythonWeb scrapingBeautifulSouppandasComposite index
Impact
A holistic view of child development in the state and a clear criterion for prioritizing the municipalities where children are most vulnerable.

04 · São Paulo State · Early childhood

São Paulo Early Childhood Datapedia

Role
  • The Datapedia grew out of São Paulo's state-wide early childhood diagnosis, with the goal of bringing together the indicators produced in one place and making them easy to consult and reuse.
  • Built rates, proportions and coverage measures from public databases, such as prenatal care visits and vaccination, and consolidated the indicators into interactive maps by municipality and region.
  • Analyzed the indicators' time series from 2010 to 2024 and the inequalities across territories and by race, interpreting the results in light of the state's socio-historical context.
  • Realized that all this material would be confined to a Word report and developed, as an extra deliverable, an HTML product that works as a browsable repository of the maps, charts and databases used.
Tools
PythonpandasFoliumPlotlyGeoJSONTime seriesHTML/CSS
Impact
The diagnosis went beyond the document: the client gained an interactive, reusable collection of indicators and visualizations on early childhood in the state.
